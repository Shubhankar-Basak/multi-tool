RPM (Revenue per Mille) is your estimated earnings per 1,000 pageviews. It's calculated by dividing total earnings by pageviews and multiplying by 1,000.

Click-through rate is total clicks divided by total impressions, multiplied by 100 to express it as a percentage.

Cost per click is the average amount earned each time a visitor clicks an ad — total earnings divided by total clicks.
