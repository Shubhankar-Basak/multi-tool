What is the Monthly Revenue Calculator?

The Monthly Revenue Calculator projects longer-term earnings from a single daily figure. Enter what your site earns on a typical day, and it instantly extrapolates that into weekly, monthly, and yearly revenue projections — useful for budgeting, forecasting, or simply understanding what a day's earnings mean at scale.

Why This Calculator Matters

Daily AdSense earnings are the number most publishers see first, but they're hard to reason about in isolation. Turning a daily figure into weekly, monthly, and yearly projections makes it far easier to compare against costs like hosting, content production, or advertising spend, and to set realistic longer-term revenue goals.

The Formula

Weekly Revenue = Daily Earnings × 7
Monthly Revenue = Daily Earnings × 30
Yearly Revenue = Daily Earnings × 365

Each projection is a straightforward linear extrapolation, assuming daily earnings stay consistent across the period. It's a planning estimate, not a guarantee — actual results will vary with traffic and seasonality.

Worked Example

At $35 per day, projected revenue is $35 × 7 = $245 per week, $35 × 30 = $1,050 per month, and $35 × 365 = $12,775 per year.

How to Use This Calculator

  • Daily Earnings: Use a representative average daily figure rather than your single best or worst day.

For the most reliable projection, average your last 30 days of earnings and divide by 30 to get a stable daily figure before entering it here.

Tips for More Reliable Projections

Use a trailing 30-day average for daily earnings rather than a single day, which smooths out normal day-to-day volatility.

Recalculate seasonally, since Q4 advertiser demand often lifts daily earnings well above the yearly average.

Treat the yearly figure as directional — traffic, CTR, and CPC rarely stay perfectly flat for a full 365 days.

Common Mistakes

The most common mistake is projecting from an unusually strong single day, which inflates every downstream figure. Weekends and weekdays can also differ meaningfully in traffic and CPC for many niches, so a daily figure taken from only one day of the week may not represent a typical day well.
